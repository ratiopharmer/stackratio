Bloodwork on cycle

Which values people check on cycle, why they change, when to test and how to read the report.

When users test

Before
A full baseline panel a few weeks before starting. Without it you cannot tell later what the compounds changed and what was already there.
On cycle
Around 6–8 weeks in, when most values have settled at their new level – and again on longer runs.
After
A few weeks after stopping, to see whether values come back to baseline and the body's own production restarts.

Hematocrit and hemoglobin

Testosterone and most other compounds push red blood cell production. Hematocrit and hemoglobin rise, the blood gets thicker. Users watch it closely: high hematocrit raises blood pressure and the risk of clots. Dehydration on the day of the test makes it look higher than it is.

Lipids: HDL, LDL, triglycerides

Almost every compound lowers HDL – oral ones, trenbolone and some others very strongly – while LDL often goes up. The ratio between the two says more than either number. Lipids change slowly and recover slowly, so many keep cardio, diet and omega-3 going on cycle.

Liver values: ALT, AST, GGT

ALT and AST are the classic liver markers, and orals are the main reason they climb. A catch: hard training raises both too, because muscle releases them as well. Users therefore look at GGT as a cross-check and avoid heavy training in the days before the test.

Estradiol

Testosterone converts partly into estradiol (E2). Too high and too low both cause problems – water, mood, libido, joints. Users read the number together with total testosterone and how they feel. Ask the lab for the sensitive assay if possible.

Testosterone, LH and FSH

Total and free testosterone show what is in the blood on cycle. LH and FSH show the body's own production – they drop close to zero on cycle and are the key values after stopping, when the question is whether the natural axis comes back.

Prolactin

Prolactin comes up mostly with the 19-nor compounds such as nandrolone and trenbolone. Elevated values are linked to libido and erection problems and are a common reason users add it to the panel when those compounds are involved.

Kidney values

Creatinine and the eGFR calculated from it estimate kidney function. More muscle mass and creatine both raise creatinine without anything being wrong, so the value can look worse than the kidneys are. Cystatin C is less affected by muscle and gives a clearer picture.

Not in the blood, but on the list

Blood pressure is measured at home, not in the lab – and it is one of the most important numbers on cycle. A hematocrit or lipid problem often shows up here first.

Reading your report

  • Reference ranges differ between labs. Always compare a value with the range printed on your own report.
  • One value is not a diagnosis. Trends between tests say more than a single result.
  • Context matters. Training, hydration, time since the last injection and time of day all move the numbers.
  • Far outside the range – or symptoms like chest pain, headaches or shortness of breath – is a reason to see a doctor, not to wait for the next panel.
